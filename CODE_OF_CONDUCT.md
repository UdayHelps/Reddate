# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. This is a small project run by volunteers, and everyone here is giving their time.

- Treat people with respect. No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Disagree with ideas, not people. Assume good intent and ask before you assume otherwise.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic, and be patient: replies can take a few days.
- Don't post other people's private information, keys, tokens or addon links.

Reports of unacceptable behaviour can go to support@reddate.xyz. The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them.
