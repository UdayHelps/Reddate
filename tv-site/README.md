# Reddate TV

The Reddate app for Android TV. Dark, warm, made for the remote. Accent colour orange (#ff6a32) on near-black (#141110), cream text (#f6f1ea).

## The four things worth showing

### 1. Watch alone, or together
Pick a title and the TV asks "How do you want to watch?": **Watch alone** or **Watch together**.
In a party, the host opens **Invite** and chooses **Invite all my profiles** (or one profile, or another TV).
On the other profile's TV a pop-up appears: **"Me invites you to watch together" — Join / Not now**.
Press Join and they are in the same scene at the same second. A toast and the "Who's here" list show who joined.
No seat limit. People outside the account join with a short party code.

### 2. Chat from the couch
Everyone in the party can chat on the TV (with the TV's on-screen keyboard). Friends argue about what to watch next:
- "Bro please play Stranger Things next"
- "PLEASE NO"
Chat shows over the picture and can be hidden.

### 3. Tired of choosing streams every time?
Normally every episode needs you to scroll a long list of streams: different addons, qualities, languages.
Reddate TV remembers the one you chose (addon, quality, source, language, format). The next episode starts it by itself with
"Same as last time" and marks matches **★ Your usual**.

### 4. Continue watching, same provider
The Home row resumes at the exact second with the same stream kind, and "Up next" starts the following episode.
