Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Reddate YT and the YouTube key are both free. YouTube gives every key a free allowance each day.

Is my key safe?

It is encrypted into your personal link and used only to ask YouTube for the lists you chose. Keep the link to yourself, because it works like the key.

It says YouTube isn't turned on for my key

Do step 1 again: press Enable, wait a minute, then press Install addon.

It says the key isn't valid

Copy the whole key again from the Credentials page. It starts with AIza and has no spaces.
