    What's an addon? Tap to find out

    Think of Reddate as an empty TV. It has the screen and the sound, but no channels.

    An addon is like a channel pack you plug in. Each one is a tiny helper that knows where to look: one for trending movies, one for YouTube, one for subtitles.

    You choose which helpers to plug in. Reddate asks them what they found, shows it on your Home, and plays it when you press Play. Take a helper out and its things disappear. Reddate never stores or hosts the videos.

    Questions

    What do I need?

    An Android TV or Google TV, and a free Reddate account you sign in to from your phone.

    Does Reddate provide movies and shows?

    No. Reddate hosts no video. You add addons, small helpers that find streams, and Reddate helps you find, choose and watch together.

    Can I watch with friends in another country?

    Yes. Everyone joins the same party with a short code or link and stays on the same second, wherever they are.

    How do voice and video work?

    Open the Lounge on a phone or computer and press Voice/Video. Calls go straight between people and are encrypted, for up to 8 people at once. The TV joins the chat and shows reactions.

    Is it on the Play Store?

    No, it's an early test build. Install the APK with Downloader. It is signed with Reddate's release key, and the SHA-256 above lets you check the file.

    How many people can join a party?

    There is no limit. Profiles on your account join with one press; anyone else uses the party code or link.

    Does my phone app change?

    No. This page is about the TV app. The Reddate you already use on phone and computer stays as it is.

    Can I watch in a browser?

    Yes: open tv.reddate.xyz/app on a phone or computer. Some files (MKV, HEVC, DTS sound) play on the TV only.
