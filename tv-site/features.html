Watch together

Same scene, same second, on every screen.

  • Invite a profile, or all of themPick one profile on your account, another TV, or everyone at once. They get Join or Not now, wherever they are in the app.
  • Join with a code or a QRFriends outside your account scan the QR or open the link, sign in, pick a profile and they are in. No limit on how many.
  • The host decidesSeeking and picking what plays stay with the host. Guests can pause and play for everyone, and the host can switch that off.
  • Chat from the couchReply with the TV keyboard, hide it when you want a clean picture, and see who is in the room.
  • Gentle on slow connectionsA screen that falls behind catches up smoothly, and a dropped TV rejoins on its own.

Choosing a stream

So you never pick blind.

  • Speed you can seeEach stream is tested on your own connection, with the response time and speed shown. The best come first.
  • Formats at a glance4K, HDR, Dolby Vision and Atmos, DTS, channels and source, as small badges.
  • Languages and devicesFlags for every language in the release, and icons for whether it should play on this TV, a phone or a computer.
  • Your usualReddate remembers the kind of stream you picked for a show and uses it for the next episode, and for resume.
  • Never a black screenStreams your TV cannot decode are hidden or ranked last.

Home

Made for the remote.

  • Top 10Today's most-watched movies and series, and the leaders on each streaming service, as big ranked numbers.
  • Continue watchingPick up at the same second with the same provider. Hold OK to remove one. The next episode starts by itself.
  • Your addons, your rowsAdd catalog addons for more rows and reorder or hide any of them. Each addon shows its own logo.
  • ProfilesEveryone gets their own Home, addons and history, with a quick switch in the side menu.
  • SearchSeries and movies in their own rows, so a show is never buried under its films.

Player

Everything a TV player should do.

  • Skip with a previewFast-forward and a picture of that moment appears above the bar.
  • Subtitles your wayPick from many online options without a restart. Adjust delay, size, colour, background, font and position.
  • AudioStarts in English or your language, with flags, and you can change it any time.
  • EpisodesSwitch episode without leaving the picture.
  • UpdatesNew versions install from the side menu.
